How to avoid mud: three rules

Mud is colour that has lost its life. It happens when opposite colours are scrubbed together while wet.

01 · Let each layer dry

Blue over dry orange stays blue. Touch the canvas: if it feels cool, wait.

02 · One stroke, then lift

Going back and forth mixes the layers under your brush.

03 · Wipe between colours

Wipe on the rag between colours, rinse between mixes.
