About
A studio that designs paintings for other hands.

Geste is a small studio in Lyon. We design abstract works stroke by stroke, then break them into layers anyone can follow.

[Photo: the studio table, brushes, a canvas mid-layer]
01
Designed, then painted
Every work is composed as a sequence of gestures, and painted by the studio before it is released.
02
Honest previews
The digital preview is labelled as such, and every work shows a real result by a first-time painter.
03
Yours, not ours
Your painting will not look like the preview. It will look like you. That is the point.
