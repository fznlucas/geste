The plan

Three layers, each one on a dry canvas. The first sets the warmth, the second places the big blue masses and the dark line, the third adds light and a few marks. Keep this page open next to you.

01 · Underlayer
Peach ground, warm yellow and sea green, top left.
02 · Gestures
Turquoise and sky bottom left, ultramarine and night top right, one dark line.
03 · Veils & marks
White veils in the middle, orange sparks, dark marks bottom right.
