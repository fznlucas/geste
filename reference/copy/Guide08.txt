Avoid mud
Clean
Blue laid over dry orange. Both colours stay alive.
Mud
Blue scrubbed into wet orange. They cancel into grey-brown.
If something goes wrong
It turned grey
Let it dry completely, then paint the colour again on top, in one stroke.
Too dark
A dry-brushed veil of white lifts it without covering it.
Too busy
Cover one area with peach ground. Empty space makes the rest breathe.
It looks unfinished
It is probably done.
