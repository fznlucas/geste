How Geste works

Every work is designed stroke by stroke, then broken down into layers anyone can follow. You do not need to know how to paint. You need an afternoon.

01
Choose
A work, a format, a palette. The format sets the level, or pick your own.
02
Get
Your guide unlocks in your library, with the exact shopping list for your canvas.
03
Paint
Three layers, step by step, on your phone. Works offline, with drying timers.
04
Sign
Stop, sign, hang it. It will not look like the preview. It will look like yours.
The size sets the level
Bigger canvases need more layers and more gestures. You can also go custom: advanced technique on a small canvas.

Questions
I have never painted. Is that a problem?
That is who Geste is for. Start with a Beginner work: two layers, flat strokes, about an hour.
Which paint do I need?
Every guide comes with an exact shopping list for your format and palette, with a cheaper alternative for each item.
Can I use it without internet?
Yes. Open the guide once online and it stays on your phone.
Can I print the guide?
Yes, three times per guide. Each copy carries your name.
What if mine looks different?
It will. That is the point. Stop earlier than you think.
Ready for your first canvas?
Start with a Beginner work: two layers, about an hour.
