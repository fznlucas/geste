Veils & marks

30 min · then stop25 mm flat · round n°6
Your canvas after this layer. Faded strokes are the layers already dry.
On the plate
WhiteSkyOrangePayne's greyNight
aLoad the 25 mm brush with white and almost no water. Drag it lightly across the middle right: it should skip over the texture.bAdd two thin vertical sparks of orange, below the veil.cWith the round brush, place a few dark marks in the bottom right: a short line, a hook, a small curve. Payne's grey, straight from the tube.dFor a drip: thin a drop of night with water, touch the canvas, let gravity do the rest.eStop. Put the brushes in water and leave the room for ten minutes before deciding anything.
TipIf you want to add more, add less. Most paintings are ruined in the last fifteen minutes.
