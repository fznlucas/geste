Underlayer

30 min · then dry 30 min50 mm flat
Your canvas after this layer. Faded strokes are the layers already dry.
On the plate
Peach groundWarm yellowYellowOrangeSea green
aCover most of the canvas with the peach ground in wide, loose strokes. Leave a few gaps of raw canvas near the edges.bWhile it is still wet, brush the warm yellow across the left side, a little above the middle. Two or three strokes, slightly curved.cAdd a patch of orange at the top, in the middle. It will peek through later.dWith the edge of the brush, scratch a few diagonal strokes of sea green in the top left corner.eWash the brush. Let it dry: 30 minutes, or 10 with a hair dryer on cool.
TipThin is good. This layer should look almost like watercolour. If the paint feels thick, add a little water to the plate, not to the canvas.
