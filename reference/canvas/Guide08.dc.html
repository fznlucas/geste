Avoid mud

CleanBlue laid over dry orange. Both colours stay alive.
MudBlue scrubbed into wet orange. They cancel into grey-brown.
If something goes wrong
It turned greyLet it dry completely, then paint the colour again on top, in one stroke. Too darkA dry-brushed veil of white lifts it without covering it. Too busyCover one area with peach ground. Empty space makes the rest breathe. It looks unfinishedIt is probably done.
