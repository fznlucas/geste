Your canvas after this layer. Faded strokes are the layers already dry.
On the plate
TurquoiseSkySea greenUltramarineNight
aBottom left: four wide horizontal strokes, alternating turquoise and sky. Push, do not paint twice.bWith the 25 mm brush, cross them with short diagonal strokes of sky. Like quick hatching.cTop right: fill a large block with ultramarine, then drag night down in two vertical strokes over it.dLoad the round brush with night. In one breath, draw the long line: up from the left edge, then across the top, landing in the dark mass.eStep back two metres. If it already feels balanced, skip to the tip. Let it dry.
TipThe dark line is the only moment to go slowly. Rest your little finger on the canvas edge to steady your hand, then keep moving.
